Active Heat

Make risky contract resolutions carry a legible, recoverable cost in the existing seven-contract, game-time-driven prototype.

Design only. Authored contract pressure, a 24-hour Go to Ground action removing one Heat, and removal of the unused Alerts field/button are approved directions—not implemented features. Review this design before the implementation plan.

Player rules

Heat and exposure

Heat starts at 2, stays nonnegative, and increases only through authored contract responses. There is no automatic decay; ordinary Rest does not reduce Heat. Exposure labels are derived from Heat, not saved as a second meter.

HeatExposureOn upward crossing
0–2LOWNo warning
3–5ELEVATEDAt 3
6–8WATCHEDAt 6
9+CRITICALAt 9

Each newly crossed threshold sends one distinct SYSTEM message and highlighted ticker warning, in ascending order. A jump across two sends both. Falling Heat and reloading send none; crossing again after recovery can warn again. Contract-resolution feedback stays. Heat is uncapped, and CRITICAL remains the label above 9. Warnings describe surveillance pressure, not a raid, sweep, credit penalty, or forced move.

Contract choices

Data Retrieval keeps its existing swap: spoof_credentials at Heat ≤3 and routed_vendor_id at Heat ≥4. The prepared work order and always-available responses keep their rules. Visible choices and direct resolution requests must agree.

Silent Partner (M-613): at Heat ≤5, buy_silence pays 900 CR out of the 5,600-CR reward, yielding 4,700 CR and Heat +0. At Heat ≥6, replace it with authored buy_intermediary_silence: pay 1,300 CR out of the reward, yielding 4,300 CR and Heat +0. It completes the contract with no standing/favor changes or successor unlocks; its preview, result, and message explain the intermediary needed under scrutiny. mirror_archive (+5,600 CR, Heat +2) and abort remain available at every Heat. No global price multiplier or other changed contract outcomes.

Go to Ground

Home offers GO TO GROUND // 24 HOURS // HEAT -1 when Heat >0 and no contract is active. One use advances exactly 1,440 game minutes through normal clock, deadline, and rent settlement; then it removes one Heat and saves. It directly costs no Credits, grants no standing/favor, moves no residence, and refunds no expired opportunity. Due or overdue rent does not prevent it. With Heat 0 or an active contract, reject with no mutation. Existing Rest keeps its midnight timing and active-contract restriction.

Home shows the exact Heat number and exposure label alongside this action and its time cost. Disable the button when unusable; hide it during residence confirmation. No new panel, modal, or status-chip redesign.

Remove Alerts

Remove the inert rail item and its automatic separator, Home count, GameState signal/state, and serialized field. Heat becomes the only pressure measure; do not add an Alerts scene.

State & data flow

  • GameState owns mutations. Resolution applies authored Heat, then publishes warnings on upward crossings. Existing heat_changed, messages_changed, and ticker_message signals update Home, Comms, and ticker. Comms refreshes while open when a message arrives; no message threads or read actions.
  • get_contract() returns choice snapshots filtered by _available_choices(); resolve_contract() enforces the same filter. Contract detail displays authored previews without computing prices.
  • Home sends a Go to Ground intent through Main to a guarded GameState action. Advance 1,440 minutes first through _advance_minutes(), including offer expiry and rent; then lower Heat by one, publish a recovery ticker/SYSTEM message, and save once. No active job can use the action, but published unaccepted offers can expire during it.
  • Previously saved version-4 profiles and supported earlier migrations still load. Ignore a legacy alerts key and omit it on new saves; no version bump is required because no required field is added. If an old profile selects alerts as its active module, select home on load. Preserve other module handling and persist Heat, messages, clock, contracts, and rent. Loading sends no threshold warning and removes no Heat.

What must be verified

  • 2→4, 5→7, and 8→10 warn only for newly crossed bands; save/reload does not replay warnings. Falling or unchanged Heat is silent; lowering and crossing again warns again.
  • At Heat 5 versus 6, Silent Partner shows the correct choices and previews. Directly requesting a hidden option fails without mutation. The high-Heat response pays exactly 4,300 CR with Heat +0, unchanged standing/favor; other responses still work.
  • Go to Ground advances exactly 24 hours even one minute before midnight; ordinary rent and offer deadlines settle. It removes one Heat and persists. At zero Heat or with active work it changes nothing. Rest never reduces Heat.
  • Old profiles containing alerts, including one with active module alerts, keep gameplay data and save without the obsolete field. Rail has no dead Alerts button; Comms updates on a warning while already open.

Explicit boundaries

No raids, sweeps, random rolls, persistent alert level, new contract phases, generic repricing, new housing tiers, extra favors, new audio assets, or map/district simulation. Missed contract deadlines still apply no Heat penalty. Warnings change neither deadlines nor payouts.
