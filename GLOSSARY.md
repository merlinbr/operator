# Operator

Language for the operator's work and relationships in the local world.

## Language

**Venue**:
A named local place with people and authored opportunities for the operator to interact with. A venue is not merely a contract destination label or a backdrop.
_Avoid_: District (when referring to an individual place)

**Visit**:
The operator's personal presence at a venue to meet people and engage with local opportunities. A visit is distinct from contacting someone remotely or carrying out a contract.
_Avoid_: Remote access (when referring to a visit)

**Published contract**:
A contract offer released to the operator for consideration, with its deadline running. Publication is distinct from accepting the job.
_Avoid_: Accepted contract (when referring to an offer)
